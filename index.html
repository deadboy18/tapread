What This Page Reads

Only the NFC serial number (UID) — a 4-byte identifier broadcast by every NFC card without any authentication. Your card data, balance, and transactions cannot be accessed through a web browser. For full TNG card reading, use TapRead on Android.

Privacy

Everything happens locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server. This page has no analytics, no cookies, no tracking. You can verify by viewing the page source — it's a single HTML file.
